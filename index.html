Education & Prevention

Phishing is a broad, automated attack sending generic emails to thousands of people (e.g., "Dear Customer, reset your password").

Spear Phishing is highly targeted. The attacker uses personal information found online to make the email seem legitimate (e.g., "Hi Matheus, attached is the invoice for your recent purchase at Hi-Fi Corp").

Defense: Never click links in unsolicited emails. Verify directly by typing the official website URL into your browser.

Scammers manipulate human psychology. Common tactics include:
  • Urgency/Fear: "Your account will be deleted in 24 hours."
  • Authority: Pretending to be the IRS, Police, or a company CEO.
  • Greed: "You have an unclaimed inheritance or lottery prize."
Defense: Pause and verify. Legitimate organizations will not pressure you into immediate, panicked action.
